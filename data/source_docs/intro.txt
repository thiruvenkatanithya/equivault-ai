# STOCK MARKET FUNDAMENTALS (LEVEL: BASIC)

## What is the Stock Market?
The stock market is a public marketplace where shares of publicly held companies are issued, bought, and sold. When you buy a share, you are buying a piece of ownership in that company.

## Key Terminology
1. Bull Market: A period of rising stock prices, generally characterized by optimism and investor confidence.
2. Bear Market: A period of falling stock prices, typically defined as a drop of 20% or more from recent highs.
3. Market Capitalization: The total value of all a company's shares. Calculated as: (Current Share Price) x (Total Number of Shares).
4. Dividends: A portion of company profits paid out to shareholders.

## How to Start Investing
For beginners, the safest way to enter the market is through Index Funds or ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds). These provide instant diversification by owning a small piece of many companies instead of betting on just one.
